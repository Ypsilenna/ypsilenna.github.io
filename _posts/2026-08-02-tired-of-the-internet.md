I think I have had too much internet lately, and it started to make me feel a little bad, so I am currently limiting my online activity and spending more time on doing other things.
I think I will keep working on Little Star and the City of Wonders today, maybe on Feng: Before Dusk too, but until now I was just gaming. In the last week I didn't really do anything like that and spent most of my time online, which isn't great.

I also recently wrote a silly fanfic about AI. You can see it on my Ao3 profile (the link is on the main page.)

I remembered that I owned Smalland and The Witcher 3, so I played both. I played Echoes of the End as well, but that's a game I bought because GOG gave me an amazing coupon for it, and I bought it for cheap...

Playing Smalland made me think that insects are only little to non-scary because they are tiny and they would be absolutely terrifying if they were our size. The game is fun, though, and I finally got some mounts! My favorite one so far is the grasshopper. It makes me feel like I am Sonic X, lol

The Witcher 3 is fun. I used to play The Witcher 1 back in the day, but it's super clunky and buggy, so I don't play it too often. The third one feels much more polished (although I still encountered some problems with it.)
My favorite quest in the first zone was a side quest with a granny who wanted her frying pan back. There's nothing better than quests fitting a fantasy setting, lmao

Echoes of the End is very pretty; the story is basic, but I can unlock costumes, so there's still something for me in it. I am a bit ashamed of myself, though. I beat the first boss in several attempts, and THEN I remembered I had a self-heal. Yeah, it would've been handy to know before...

Anyway, I will go spend some more time on things I actually want to do and a little less on the internet. Cheers!
