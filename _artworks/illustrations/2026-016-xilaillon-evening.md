(not) fun fact: I had such a long break from traditional art that I forgot how to use my tools properly and nearly destroyed this piece. You don't see it here, but the paper is slightly torn in some parts ;_;

The colors aren't 100% right, but trying to adjust the colors of characters with a skintone this dark is painful, and I don't have any more energy to deal with it. These colors are good enough, so...

This is my OC, Xivaillon. I wanted to draw something more ambitious, but I figured I needed more practice after not touching traditional since I think January. 

The tools I used were pretty much everything I had in my art stash. Gouache, colored pencils, alcohol markers, acrylic pens, glitter pens, ink pens, you name it. I have a difficulty sticking to one type of materials because I want to use EVERYTHING, lol
