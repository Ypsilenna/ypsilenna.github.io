But, not to make it all about my rage over an evaporated comic, I want to tell you a bit about my current progress on Little Star and the City of Wonders.

I am currently working on chapter number 4. The final number of chapters will be either 8 or 9, so I am basically halfway there. If I speed up a bit, I will be able to finish it this year, which would make me finish two books in one year, yippee!

At the time of writing this, I am at 2300 words, but I think I will write more a little later today. I think I am finally immersed in the story properly, and it's a lot of fun.

I will have to pick the next novel when I approach the end of this one, but I am not sure yet if I will go for Divorce by Magic or When You're Evil.

Both of them are dramedies, but their tone varies.

Divorce by Magic is about a depressed middle-aged writer who becomes a werewolf, and his wife threatens that she will divorce him if he doesn't cure it, but the affliction makes him happy. He makes an unfortunate wish and ends up magically married to a chaos incarnate fashionista genie for a month, which makes him lose control of his life.

When You're Evil is a little heavier, but I like the concept too. It's about a satyr floramancer who spent his entire life in his homeland, where other satyrs treated him as inferior because they didn't see him as manly enough. He moves into a human town where he ends up accidentally joining two hot elf villainesses who serve the dragons. He has a mutual crush on one of them, but he is so oblivious that he still wouldn't realize even on their wedding day.

I am slightly more gravitated towards When You're Evil at this point, but it's also supposed to be longer than Divorce by Magic. I am not sure if I can go for a longer story after going through The Snake Queen. We shall see.
